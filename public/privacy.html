Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

What Save Video is

Save Video is a free website that lets you preview a video from a link you paste and save it in a quality you choose. The Save Video browser extension is a companion that lets you save a video playing on a page with one click.

What we collect

Nothing. The website has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking. It does not store the links you paste or the files you save. Videos you save are downloaded straight from the video's own server to your device — they do not pass through our server.

What the extension collects

Nothing. The extension works entirely in your browser: it finds the video already playing on the page you are on and saves it to your device. It does not read your browsing history, your passwords or your logins, and it does not send any data to us or to anyone else. It does not read or send your cookies.

Cookies

The website itself uses no cookies for tracking. Your browser may keep a theme choice (dark or light) on your own device only.

Third parties

When you paste a link, the website asks the public page of that video's own platform (for example TikTok, Facebook, Instagram or YouTube) for its title, thumbnail and available qualities, so it can show them to you. No personal data is sent to those platforms.

Advertising

We may show ads from Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices. See our Cookie Policy for more detail.
