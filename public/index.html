Install the Save Video extension

What it does: the Save Video extension saves any video playing in your browser with one click — no need to copy and paste links. Open a video on TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and more, click the Save Video button, and it saves the best-quality video straight to your device.

Note: this works on desktop Chrome (PC or Mac) — Chrome on phones cannot install extensions.

  1. The extension file just downloaded — unzip it to a folder.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.

Once it is installed, the Install button disappears on its own — the site can tell the extension is there. It never sends your logins anywhere.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is Save Video?

    Save Video is a free online tool that works as a YouTube video downloader, TikTok video downloader, Facebook video downloader, Instagram video downloader and Twitter video downloader. It also works with Douyin, Bluesky, Tumblr, Twitch clips, Pinterest, Moj, ShareChat, LinkedIn, Snapchat and Tencent Video. Paste a video link, preview the clip, and save it in the quality you choose.

    Is Save Video free?

    Yes. Save Video is completely free to use, with no account and no software to install.

    What qualities can I save a video in?

    It depends on the video itself. Most videos offer 1080p, 720p, 480p and 360p with sound, plus an audio-only option. You only ever get the qualities the original video actually has.

    Do I need an account?

    No. You never need an account, and you never need to log in to any social media account to use Save Video.

    Does it work on my phone?

    Yes. Save Video works in any modern browser on Android, iPhone, tablet and PC.
