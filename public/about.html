What Save Video is

Save Video is a free website that lets you preview a video from a link you paste and save it in the quality you choose. It works with TikTok, Douyin, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X (Twitter), Bluesky, Tumblr, Twitch clips, Pinterest, Moj, ShareChat, LinkedIn, Snapchat and Tencent Video.

How it works

Copy a video link from any supported platform, paste it into the box on the home page, and the website shows you the video's title, thumbnail and available qualities. When you pick a quality, the video is downloaded straight from that platform's own server to your device — it does not pass through our server.

No accounts, no software

There are no accounts and no sign-in. You do not need to install anything to use the website.

Our promise

We keep the tool free and simple, we collect no personal data, and we do not store the links you paste or the files you save. Save Video is run as a small independent project — if something does not work, tell us and we will fix it.
