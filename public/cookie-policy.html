Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device by your browser. Websites use them to remember choices, measure visits and show advertising.

Cookies from Save Video itself

Save Video uses no cookies for tracking and no analytics cookies. The only thing your browser may keep is your own theme choice (dark or light), stored on your own device so the website remembers it next time. The website has no accounts and no sign-in.

Advertising cookies (Google AdSense)

We may show ads from Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

Other third parties

When you paste a link, the website asks the public page of that video's own platform (for example TikTok, Facebook, Instagram or YouTube) for its title, thumbnail and available qualities. Those platforms may set their own cookies under their own policies when your browser talks to them directly — for example when a video preview or download is fetched from their servers.

Your choices

You can control cookies in your browser settings: block them, delete them, or ask to be notified when one is set. Blocking cookies will not stop Save Video from working.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
