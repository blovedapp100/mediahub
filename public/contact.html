Email us

Write to us at blovedapp100@gmail.com. We read every message and reply as soon as we can — usually within a few days.

What to write about

You can contact us about anything to do with Save Video, for example:

— a question about the website;
— a video link or platform that does not work;
— a suggestion or feedback;
— a copyright concern or DMCA notice (see the Disclaimer page for what to include).

Before you write

If a video does not download, please include the link you pasted and what the website showed you — it helps us find the problem faster. Please do not send passwords or any personal account details; we will never ask for them.
